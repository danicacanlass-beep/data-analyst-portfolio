# Superstore Sales Analysis

First real project I've done in Excel. I'm an IT student, and data analytics is actually part of that field, so I wanted to focus on it more and build actual skills. Grabbed a sample sales dataset (about 180 orders) to practice everything from scratch — cleaning data, pivot tables, charts, the whole thing.

Data was messy when I started. Same region typed different ways (East, east, EAST), a few duplicate rows in there, some blank sales numbers, dates that Excel wasn't even reading as real dates. Had to fix all that before doing anything useful with it.

## What's in here
- Cleaned data (duplicates, spelling, blanks, dates — basically all the annoying stuff)
- Pivot tables: sales by region, profit by category, sales by month
- A profit margin calculation, since raw profit numbers alone don't tell the full story
- Two charts to actually see the region and monthly patterns

## A few things I noticed
South region had the highest sales overall, around $39.5K — beat out West, East, and North.

Furniture caught my eye. Its profit dollars looked similar to Technology's, but the margin was actually the lowest of the three at 10.2%. So it's selling fine, just not very efficiently compared to the other categories.

There's also a big jump in July sales, then noticeable dips in April and September. No idea why since it's sample data, but that's exactly the kind of thing you'd want to look into if it were real.
